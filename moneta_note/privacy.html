Moneta Note

Privacy Policy | Last Updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToTheVang ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Moneta Note mobile application.

1. Local-First Notes

Moneta Note stores your notes, drafts, settings, and media on your device by default. We do not operate a separate server that receives or stores your notes.

2. Optional Google Drive Backup and Sync

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app uses the Google account you select to back up and synchronize your notes, protected-note configuration, media, and in-app reward data to that account's private Google Drive application-data area. These files are not shown in the normal Google Drive file list. Your Google account email is stored locally only to show which account is connected.

Protected note content and protected image data remain encrypted before they are uploaded. Your password is not uploaded. You can disconnect Google Drive at any time in the app.

3. Optional Voice Input

When you choose voice input, the app requests microphone permission and uses Android's speech-recognition service to transcribe your speech into editable note text. That service may process audio according to the privacy policy of the service provider selected on your device.

4. Optional On-Device AI

AI features require you to download or import a compatible model. The model runs on your device to help search, draft, or revise notes. We do not send note content to a ToTheVang server for these features.

5. Images and Media

Images you add to a note are stored locally and may be included in Google Drive backup only when you connect Google Drive. You may encrypt selected images in the app. Unprotected images should not be treated as secure simply because they are stored inside the app.

6. Advertising

The current production release does not display advertisements. If advertising is introduced in a future release, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated before that release.
