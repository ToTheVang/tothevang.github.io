Jelly Pot

Privacy Policy | Last Updated: November 7, 2026

This policy applies to the Android closed test of Jelly Pot, developed by ToTheVang.

1. Data Collection

Jelly Pot does not require an account. The test build does not send player data to ToTheVang or other parties. It does not include live advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. Local Storage and Deletion

The game keeps progress, game records, coins, skins, preferences, and any record images you choose to create in local app storage. These files are kept until you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Copies you save elsewhere can be deleted from that location.

3. Third-Party Services

Google Play and Google Groups handle test enrollment and app distribution under Google's Privacy Policy.
